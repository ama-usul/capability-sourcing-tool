    Real company and product names appear with specs from public sources. Efforts tagged "public reporting" come from press and trade coverage and are not verified against FPDS. Front-door submissions, TRLs, vendor vehicle holdings and efforts tagged "illustrative" are invented for the demo. In production, efforts would come from contract and program data, and capabilities from each front door's submission feed, with access controlled per each source's terms and markings. AI analysis runs on the viewer's Claude account and only sees the records on this page.
